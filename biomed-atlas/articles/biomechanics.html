Introduction to Biomechanics

Understand how mechanics helps explain human movement and biological systems.

At a glance

Biomechanics applies principles of mechanics to living systems. In human movement, it can help explain how muscles, bones and joints work together to produce motion.

The science behind it

Kinematics describes motion, such as position, velocity and acceleration. Kinetics studies the forces and moments that cause or change motion. Engineers may use motion capture, force plates and computational models to study movement.

Applications and importance

Applications include sports science, gait analysis, rehabilitation, orthopaedic implant design and the development of assistive devices.

A note on interpretation

Biomechanical measurements and models have limitations. Results should be interpreted in context and, where relevant, by qualified professionals.
